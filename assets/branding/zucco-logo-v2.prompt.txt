Use case: logo-brand.
Create a fresh, professional logo concept for "zucco", a CRM and learning platform for education centers. This is a completely new design exploration.
Deliver one exceptionally polished horizontal primary logo on a clean off-white background, not a presentation board.
Design direction: confident contemporary software brand with an original compact abstract emblem and a beautifully customized lowercase "zucco" wordmark. The emblem should be a simple memorable geometric form made from two interlocking rounded shapes that suggest connection, momentum and collaboration; it must have a strong app-icon silhouette. Explore the distinctive repeated "cc" rhythm in the custom lettering, with superb optical spacing and carefully designed terminals. Mature, energetic, trustworthy and visually distinctive, suitable for a commercial SaaS product.
Use a tasteful distinctive color treatment for the emblem and deep dark lettering. Flat vector-like artwork, crisp edges, balanced proportions, generous clear space. Logo and typography should be the main focus, large enough to inspect.
Exact text: "zucco", spelled z u c c o.
Avoid books, pages, graduation caps, school buildings, generic education clipart, swooshes, arrows, thin fragile lines, shadows, 3D effects, mockups, watermarks, slogans, extra text, or multiple logos.
